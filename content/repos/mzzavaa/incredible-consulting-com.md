The site for Incredible Consulting, an independent AI and cloud consulting practice. It explains three offers in plain order: facilitated workshops that end in a costed roadmap, prototypes on the client's own data with an evaluation harness, and delivery on the way to production.

{{< junior >}}
- First commit on 24 August 2026: a Hugo site on the shared Incredible theme, so the sister sites look like one family.
- The same day I added the legal pages that an Austrian business site needs, before anything else.
- Then services, team profiles, packages, case studies, pricing and an FAQ, each as its own content section.
- The disciplines get display names from one data file, while the content folders keep their plain names. Nice words on screen, boring paths in git.
{{< /junior >}}

{{< senior >}}
Six commits between 24 August and 4 September 2026, and quiet since, which is fine for a business site that says what it needs to say. Getting the legal pages in on day one is the right order. The README is unusually clear: what the practice sells, and just as useful, what it does not sell. The theme lives in its own repo and is pinned to a specific commit, so a theme change cannot surprise this site.
